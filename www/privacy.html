Bubble Tap – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Bubble Tap is a game made by [YOUR NAME] for children and adults, including people who stim. This page explains what happens to information when you play it.

The short version

Bubble Tap does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. It works completely offline.

Information kept on your device

The game saves a few things on the device itself so they are still there next time: your level number, your best Bubble Rush scores and your settings (sound, colors, calm motion and similar). This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's site data for the web version, removes it.

Children

Bubble Tap is designed to be safe for children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13. It contains no links to other websites, no chat and no way to contact other players.

Permissions

On Android the game may use vibration for gentle taps. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
